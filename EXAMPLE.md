# Project brief: Crotchet

> **This is a made-up example.** Crotchet is not a real product or company. It was invented to
> show how much detail a brief needs, and any likeness to a real product is accidental. The
> blank template is in [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md).

## 1. Summary

Crotchet is for self-employed music teachers with 15 to 40 pupils. It lets parents book, move
and pay for lessons from one link, so the teacher stops arranging the week by text message.

## 2. The problem

The first user is a piano or guitar teacher who works alone, teaching at home or travelling to
pupils. Today the timetable lives in a paper diary or a phone calendar. Changes are arranged by
text message, one parent at a time, and payments are chased the same way.

It is painful because one cancelled lesson means several messages, the freed slot usually stays
empty, and asking a parent for a late payment is awkward. Teachers tell us this takes an
evening a week.

## 3. What we need to learn

- Teachers will set up their week in the product without help from us.
- Parents will use a link to move a lesson, and not text the teacher.
- Teachers will pay a monthly fee once they have used it for a full term.

## 4. Users and roles

- **Teacher.** Sets the weekly timetable, sees bookings and payments, messages parents.
- **Parent** (or an adult pupil). Books, moves and pays for lessons. No password: they sign in
  from a link sent by email.
- **Our admin.** One person on our team who can see every teacher account, help with problems
  and switch an account off.

## 5. Main journey

1. A teacher signs up and enters the hours they teach each week and the length of a lesson.
2. They add their pupils, with a parent's email address for each.
3. Each parent gets a link and picks a regular weekly slot for the term.
4. The teacher sees the full week on one screen.
5. A parent who cannot make a lesson opens the link, cancels it and picks any free slot in the
   next two weeks.
6. The teacher gets one notification. Nobody sends a text message.
7. At the end of the month, each parent gets one bill for the lessons taken and pays by card.

Rough paper sketches of the week screen and the parent's booking screen are attached.

## 6. Features

**Must launch:**

- Teacher sign-up and sign-in
- Weekly timetable with lesson lengths and breaks
- Pupil list with a parent contact for each pupil
- Parent link: choose a regular slot, cancel a lesson, move a lesson
- A cancellation rule set by the teacher (for example, no changes within 24 hours)
- Email notifications to the teacher and the parent for every change
- A monthly bill for each parent, paid by card
- Admin panel: list of teachers, view an account, switch an account off

**Launch if cheap:**

- A calendar feed the teacher can add to their phone calendar
- A reminder email to parents the day before a lesson
- A waiting list for a slot that is taken

**Version two:**

- Group lessons
- Lesson notes and practice tasks for pupils
- Several teachers under one music school
- An app in the app stores
- Text message reminders

## 7. Platforms

A web app that works well on a phone, for teachers and parents. We are not sure whether
teachers will expect an app in the app stores. Please recommend.

## 8. Integrations and existing systems

- **Card payments.** We have not chosen a provider. Please recommend one. We do not want to
  store card details ourselves.
- **Email.** For sign-in links, notifications and bills.
- **Calendar feed.** In "launch if cheap".
- **Existing systems.** None. Teachers keep their pupil lists on paper or in a spreadsheet, so
  an import from a spreadsheet would help but is not needed for launch.

## 9. Content and data

- We will write all the text in the product, the emails and the help page, two weeks before
  launch.
- We will supply the terms of use and the privacy policy, checked by a lawyer, two weeks before
  launch.
- Five teachers have agreed to try the first version. We will enter their timetables with them
  in launch week.
- We have a name and a logo. We have no other brand material.

## 10. Products we like

- https://calendly.com: booking from one link, with no account needed for the person booking.
- https://www.gov.uk: plain words, one question per screen, works on any phone.

## 11. Constraints

- **Budget range:** *left out of this example on purpose. In a real brief, give a real range.
  It lets an agency propose what fits.*
- **Target launch:** four weeks before the autumn term starts. Teachers set their timetables
  then, and will not change tools in the middle of a term.
- **Rules:** the product holds children's names and parents' contact details, so data
  protection law applies. We want to collect as little as possible: no dates of birth, no home
  addresses.

## 12. Ownership and support

The code, the designs, the domain and every account (hosting, email, payments) must be in our
company's name from the start, with the agency invited as a user.

After launch we expect a period of bug fixes, then a few days of work each month while we learn
from the first teachers. We have no developers of our own yet.

## 13. Decisions

The founder approves designs and answers questions, within one working day. A music teacher who
advises us joins a review call once a week. Nobody else needs to sign anything off.
