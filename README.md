# Software project brief template

A short template for describing a software product to an agency or a developer, so that the
quotes you get back can be compared.

A brief has one job: to let someone who has never met you understand the product well enough to
ask good questions and give a realistic price. It is not a specification, and it is not a pitch
deck. Two to four pages is enough. You do not need technical knowledge to fill it in.

Made by [Tesvo Technologies](https://tesvotech.com). The full guide, with a section on what
each part is for, is here:
[How to write a software project brief](https://tesvotech.com/blog/how-to-write-a-software-project-brief).

## How to use it

1. **Copy the template.** Open [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) and copy it into a document, or
   download this repository and edit the file.
2. **Fill in each section and delete the hints.** Look at [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md) to see the
   level of detail. If a section does not apply, write "not needed" so the reader knows it was
   not forgotten.
3. **Cut it down to two to four pages.** Move every idea that is not needed on launch day to
   "Version two". A brief that describes the whole future product gets a quote for the whole
   future product.
4. **Send the same brief to every agency on your shortlist.** Ask each one to break its quote
   down by the three feature groups and to say what is not included. The best replies come back
   with questions.

## What to leave out

- Technical choices you have not had a reason to make. Describe the outcome and ask for a
  recommendation.
- Long market analysis. One paragraph is plenty.
- Every idea you have ever had. Put them in "Version two".
- Secrets you do not need to share yet. If something sensitive must be shared, ask for a
  confidentiality agreement first.
